Goal Statement

How I plan to use this portfolio, and the evidence behind the instructional design skills I am bringing into the field.

Professional Goal and Self-Assessment

My goal is to begin my career as an instructional designer, building on the M.S. in Instructional Design and Technology program at the University of Tampa and the professional experience I bring from ERP software implementation and software training.

Instructional design work calls for a mix of skills: analyzing learners and content, writing clear objectives, structuring instruction logically, building interactive eLearning in tools such as Articulate Rise 360 and Storyline 360, and revising a course based on feedback. I am still early in formally practicing several of these skills, and I want this self-assessment to be honest rather than inflated.

My strongest foundation is working directly with end users and translating complex processes into something they can learn and apply. I built it over nearly three years of ERP implementation and software training, where I trained end users on complex software processes in real workplace environments. My instructional design internship, where I supported the redesign of a Retail Management course in Articulate Rise 360, gave me hands-on experience applying a structured design process: analysis, design, implementation, evaluation, and revision. My coursework and the eLearning projects in this portfolio are where I am continuing to build fluency with Rise 360, Storyline 360, scenario-based design, and evaluation methods. These are areas I am actively developing, not areas I have mastered.

Instructional Design and Course Development

During my instructional design internship with Braathe Enterprises, I supported the redesign of an existing Retail Management course. The work moved through course analysis, design, implementation, evaluation, and revision, and I incorporated feedback into iterative improvements.

Evidence

eLearning Development with Articulate Rise 360 and Storyline 360

I have built eLearning in two Articulate 360 authoring tools. The Retail Management Course Redesign and Professional Email Communication for Workplace Success were developed in Rise 360, and the Disaster Response Simulator was built in Storyline 360. Working in both tools is helping me learn when a Rise course fits the content and when Storyline's more flexible interactions are needed.

Evidence

  • Disaster Response Simulator

    Why this is evidence: It is a Storyline 360 project built around scenarios and decision-making activities, so it shows my work in the more interactive of the two tools.

  • Retail Management Course Redesign

    Why this is evidence: I developed this course in Rise 360 during my internship.

  • Professional Email Communication for Workplace Success

    Why this is evidence: It is a second Rise 360 course, on professional email communication in the workplace.

That work involved ongoing collaboration with clients and end users, which I expect to draw on when designing training for people inside an organization.

Looking Ahead

This portfolio will keep developing alongside my coursework. I plan to publish interactive Rise 360 and Storyline 360 demos directly on the site, finish documenting Leading with Emotional Intelligence, and add new projects as I complete them. Together, the experience and projects described above are the evidence I currently have for an entry-level instructional design role: not a finished skill set, but a foundation I intend to keep building through the rest of this program and into the next stage of my career.
